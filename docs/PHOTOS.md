# The 64 photos, sorted

All pulled from the church's own website at full resolution. They are in
`research/sorted/`, grouped into folders and renamed so Finder is useful.
Nothing is on the site yet.

**Headline: only about a third are actually Baker Road.** The rest is stock art
the old website's theme shipped with — praying silhouettes, mountains, a fjord.
It is the single biggest reason the old site felt like nowhere in particular.

---

## Use these

**`03-USE-life` — the two best photos you have (2)**
- **Worship service on stage.** Real people, real room, the screen reading
  "Praise God from whom all blessings flow." A bit dark, worth a light lift.
  → homepage, and the Worship block.
- **Pumpkin Patch, ladies on a hay bale.** Three members, pumpkins, a pickup,
  a scarecrow. Warm, specific, unmistakably Baytown. → top of Outreach.

**`02-USE-building` — where we are (4)**
- **Church exterior today** — the best single image for Plan a Visit. Shows the
  building, the drive and where you'd park. Overcast, but honest.
- **Easter cross with purple drape** outside the doors, flowers around it.
  → the SONrise service on Outreach.
- **Interior renovation with scaffolding** — good for history, not for a hero.
- **Fred Hartman Bridge at sunset** — genuinely beautiful. ⚠️ **Verify who took
  it.** It may be stock the theme supplied, in which case the license probably
  did not survive leaving SnapPages. The line drawing on the homepage covers
  this ground already with no license risk.

**`04-USE-history` — the archive (6)**
These are the most valuable thing in the whole set and nothing else can replace
them. The 1980 building in black and white, two former pastors, the
congregation in the eighties, children under the old church sign.
→ the history timeline on About.
⚠️ Two of these show children. They are decades old, and the children in them
are adults now. Worth a moment's thought before publishing.

**`01-USE-people` — portraits (14)**
Studio portraits on the same mottled gray backdrop — consistent, if dated.
- **Everyone is now identified, and none of it is guesswork.** The old site's
  own captions name them, and the hand-named copies turned out to be byte-for-
  byte the same files. See **`docs/LEADERSHIP-PHOTOS.md`** for the full table,
  including six more staff whose photos we only have asset IDs for.
- Note **#0 and #53 are the same man twice** (Alan Barber) — the site needs one.
- Six are on the About page, cut out and set on a flat grey disc. Four of those
  six are photographs of a *couple*, because the church used couple photographs
  as staff portraits. `scripts/make-portraits.py` rebuilds them.
- The rest are members. Since the ~70-name committee page is being dropped,
  those have nowhere to go.

**`05-USE-logos` (9)**
- **The church logo**, at 500x411 — higher resolution than the copy that was
  embedded in the draft. Already swapped into the site header.
- Men's Crosswalk Ministry logo → Ministries.
- San Jacinto Baptist Association + SBTC → About, if you want to show affiliation.
- App Store / Google Play badges → the Watch page app link.
- **The 2023 Pumpkin Patch poster.** Do not publish it — it carries last year's
  dates. But read it: it confirms food trucks, face painting and a bounce house,
  and adds **live music, a "Bootiful Baby Contest" and "Oliver the Watusi"**.
  It also lists hours: Mon–Fri 12:30–8:30, Sat 10–8:30, Sun 12–8:30.
  **Are those still this year's hours?** If so, they belong on the site.

## Probably skip

**`06-MAYBE-stock` (7)** — competent stock that is at least on-topic: "Everyone
is welcome", a cross against blue sky, two hands reaching, a BBQ grill, the
empty tomb, a board game, water. Usable as a last resort, but every one of them
makes the site look like any other church site. **A bad photo of your actual
building beats a good photo of somebody else's.**

## Do not use

**`07-REJECT-stock-or-generic` (20)** — praying silhouettes, a man in a "Jesus
is King" shirt in the mountains, a fjord, sand dunes, string lights, an open
Bible with a houseplant. None of it is Baytown and none of it is you. Also in
here: the **gray question-mark placeholder** that is still published on the live
Crosswalk page where two officers' names should be.

**`08-REJECT-copyright-risk` (2)** — two Hurricane Harvey photographs, one of a
flooded street rescue and one aerial of a burning house in floodwater. These
look like press photographs. **Do not republish them.** Wire-service photos are
aggressively licensed, and a church is not an exempt use.
